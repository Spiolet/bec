# My First Volunteer Experience at U.S. Kids Golf

### By Bryant Cai


During the 2025 fall season, I participated in my first volunteer experience at the U.S. Kids Golf Tournament. It was an exciting and meaningful opportunity to support young golfers and learn how a large event is organized.

As a volunteer, I helped set up the medals and awards table, assisted with fundraising activities, and guided players and families to their assigned areas. I also helped place course flags during the tournament and participated in cleanup after the event.

Through this experience, I learned how much teamwork and communication are required to make a tournament run smoothly. I enjoyed meeting other volunteers and players, and I felt proud to contribute to such a positive and fun atmosphere.

Overall, volunteering at the U.S. Kids Golf Tournament taught me responsibility, cooperation, and the value of giving back to my community. I look forward to participating again in future events.
