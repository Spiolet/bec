## How Golf Changed My Personality
### Eric Jiang
#### 11-18-2025

My relationship in golf caused me to be a more positive and grateful person. I’ve only started golf for about two years and this is what I have learnt. How I turned positive is when I have a bad shot, I used to usually criticize myself for doing such a terrible shot. But after I realized, criticizing wasn’t the right thing to do, I turned my useless self talk to positive self talk. That difference made me a more complete person and let me express myself and even win a few tournaments.

What made me change is because of US Kids tournaments. In tournaments I discovered that when I have a bad temper, it leads to a horrendous shot, and because of the bad shot, it leads to more frustration. After a while, you’ll regret doubting your abilities and feel ashamed of yourself. It improves my personality in real life too, since I achieve more of the goals I want because of being so desperate and willing to succeed.

While doing tournaments, I might tell my mom that I will aim to the right since I have a natural draw. After the shot, I recognized that sometimes, it might not draw and go right into the woods. But on the other hand, it might miraculously come back and find the dead center of the fairway. When there’s water that I need to carry over to be safe, I’ll always go for it.

One shot lead. I focus too much on the scorecard or if I’m either losing or winning. In the middle of leading, I will hit a bad shot so I will get angry and that causes me to make my round worse. Once realizing that if I don’t focus on my scorecard, I will be more into my game and score well beyond what I was thinking I will shoot.
In conclusion, I would like to make the PGA, but to succeed, I will have to be a positive, grateful, honest, patient and flexible person. Make yourself positive and always only worry about your next shot that will lead you to victory.
