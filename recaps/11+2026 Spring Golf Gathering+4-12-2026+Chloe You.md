## 2026 Spring Golf Gathering
### Chloe You
#### 4-12-2026
On a beautiful spring day, Boston enjoyed mild temperatures, light breezes, and plenty of sunshine—ideal conditions for golf.

Our club, the Boston Eagles Club, hosted a 9-hole event at Blue Hill Country Club. Jayden Jiang and I invited 10 friends to participate, bringing together players from elementary, middle, and high school. With a wide range of experience levels, the event felt both dynamic and engaging.

We organized this gathering to give young golfers the opportunity to play on a high-quality course while also connecting with one another. It wasn’t just about competition—we wanted participants to exchange ideas, refine their skills, and develop a deeper appreciation for the game.

After everyone arrived, I checked in the players, and Jaden organized us into three groups of four. Once the round began, the energy on the course quickly became both focused and competitive. The opening drives set a strong tone, and each group moved steadily through the course while maintaining a spirit of friendly competition.

Not every shot went as planned. At times, I struggled with consistency, but my partner Eric offered helpful advice that allowed me to make adjustments and stay composed. That kind of support made a noticeable difference. The course also presented its own challenges, especially around the greens, where precision and control were essential.

There were moments when frustration set in after a poor shot, and it took effort to reset mentally. Still, I worked to stay focused and disciplined. Before long, the three-hour round came to an end.

Afterward, we regrouped with the other players and reflected on our rounds. Everyone shared their experiences, and the atmosphere was relaxed and upbeat. It was clear that the event had been both enjoyable and meaningful for everyone involved.

We concluded with an award ceremony. Ben delivered the top performance of the day, finishing with an impressive score of 43 strokes. Jayden Wu also had a strong showing, finishing just two strokes over par. Their performances were well deserved and widely recognized.

Overall, the event was a rewarding experience. Beyond improving our skills and gaining competitive experience, we strengthened friendships and built a stronger sense of community. Events like this highlight how golf can bring people together, and it’s an experience I will remember for a long time.

Finally, we would like to give special thanks to Ms. Wendy Hu for helping reserve the tee times at her private club and for assisting with the pace of play among our three groups. Her support helped ensure the event ran smoothly, and we are very grateful for her help in making this event possible.
