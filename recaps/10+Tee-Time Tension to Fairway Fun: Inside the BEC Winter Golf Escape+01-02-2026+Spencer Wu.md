## Tee-Time Tension to Fairway Fun: Inside the BEC Winter Golf Escape
### Spencer Haohang Wu
#### 01-02-2026

On January 2, 2026, four members of the Boston Eaglets Club (BEC), along with a few friends, gathered at TPC Prestancia for the BEC Winter Golf Escape in Sarasota. The day began with a hint of nervous energy. As tee time approached, the BEC members exchanged worried glances; the friends were nowhere to be seen. With only fifteen minutes remaining before the scheduled 1:16 PM start, doubts crept in about whether the group would be complete or rushed before the round even began. I also began to ask myself, Had I mentioned the right time in the email? Had I sent the email to begin with?Those concerns and my nervousness quickly disappeared when the friends finally arrived, ready to play. I took deep breaths and relaxed. I couldn’t focus on the round ahead. The mood instantly shifted from anxious to upbeat. As BEC Co-Manager—and “Mr. Rules Official,” as I was humorously labelled by one of the younger players—I called out the groups at the 1st tee box. Laughter replaced tension, and the round officially began on a positive note. Waiting is a constant in golf, but I definitely didn’t expect to wait so much. We had a bit of a weird grouping; I was in the last group because I was calling out the names for the tee. The order was two foursomes followed by a twosome (my dad and I). However, this taught me to be patient, realizing that not everything happens as fast as I want it to. I started to lose my patience a bit when the group in front of us had to hit multiple extra balls, but I understood that this is golf. It happens to me, and it happens to everyone else, so I calmed down and kept waiting. A few minutes turned into thirty, thirty minutes turned into an hour, and an hour turned into a few more hours. Eventually, I started to get frustrated with the waiting, but I saw that they were having fun. I realized that it might be inconvenient to wait, but I didn’t start golf to finish a round in three hours. I had picked up golf because of the journey it would take me on, hours upon hours of me talking to my friends, having fun, and learning a lot, not just from my friends, but from myself, too.Despite the early frustrations, the day unfolded into exactly what the Winter Golf Escape was meant to be: Competitive golf mixed with camaraderie, friendly banter, and shared enjoyment of a beautiful course.
