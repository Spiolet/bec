## Inspiration on the Course: A Memorable Winter Outing with   U.S. Kids Foundation’s Mr.John Kim
#### Brian Li
##### 11-22-2025
On November 16th, 2025, the Boston Eaglets Club conducted its first official winter outing at Brae Burn Country Club. More than 25 members joined us, filling the course with excitement from the moment we started at 9:30 a.m. until we wrapped up around 2:30 p.m.

One of the biggest highlights of the day was welcoming Mr. John Kim, the Senior Director of Communications and Foundation Relations at the U.S. Kids Golf Foundation. We first connected with Mr. Kim via email two years ago, and he had consistently expressed his desire to meet our club in person. Being able to finally bring him to Boston and introduce him to the Eaglets made the outing feel especially meaningful.
Before heading to the Highland Course, everyone gathered at my house to go over pairings, names, and rules. To encourage new friendships and create an inclusive environment, we paired players with people they hadn’t met before while also balancing ages and experience levels. Our club comprises a diverse community of junior golfers, ranging from experienced high school competitors who have played in top tournaments to younger members just starting their golf journey.

In my own pairing, I met two new members, Richard and Luke. I had the opportunity to learn about their goals as golfers and hear about their middle school experiences. Listening to them talk about gaining distance, getting new clubs, and grinding on the course reminded me of my own early years in golf. It brought back memories of the weekends I spent playing U.S. Kids tournaments with my dad, which days were filled with challenges, excitement, and moments I still treasure. Now that I’m a junior in high school, school life occupies most of my time, and I don’t get to relive those experiences as often. Hearing their stories gave me a sense of nostalgia and made me appreciate how far I’ve come.

After finishing our round, we all gathered for lunch at my house, accompanied by Mr. Kim. At the table, Mr. Kim shared valuable lessons and experiences with us, whether related to golf or our futures. One piece of advice in particular stayed with me: “To truly appreciate the good moments, you must experience the difficult ones.”In golf terms, you need those rough rounds so that when you finally have a great one, the feeling is that much more meaningful. His message resonated deeply with many of us around the table.

In addition, I enjoyed hearing from the other Eaglets about their aspirations and future goals. Many hoped to one day play collegiate golf, while others saw golf more as a hobby and dreamed of pursuing entirely different paths, including becoming a singer. It was inspiring to see how everyone had their own dreams, yet we were all connected through our shared love of the game.
In all, this outing was truly unforgettable. I learned a great deal from everyone who attended, and I’m grateful for the friendships, motivation, and inspiration that came from this day. Most importantly, I want to thank Mr. Kim for traveling all the way to Boston to meet our club. His advice and encouragement will stay with us for a long time, and I know this event is only the beginning of many more meaningful experiences ahead for the Boston Eaglets Club.
